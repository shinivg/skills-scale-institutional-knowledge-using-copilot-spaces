# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality assurance strategy and ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release. They partner with developers, product managers, and project managers to define test plans and validate the user-facing experience.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality strategy, testing scope, and release gates
- Create and maintain test plans for unit, integration, smoke, and manual QA activities
- Coordinate automated testing and CI validation for critical workflows
- Validate that features meet acceptance criteria before merge or release
- Report quality trends, defect patterns, and test coverage to the project team
- Participate in release readiness reviews and production follow-up

### Goals
- Ship reliable features with minimal production defects
- Enable fast feedback loops between engineering and product
- Maintain clear quality signals for project leadership and stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and review meetings
- Quality dashboards and defect triage updates
- Test plan documentation and release readiness sign-off
- Cross-team coordination with development and product leads

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to validate implementation quality and testability
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and release readiness
- Supports Project Managers by surfacing delivery risk, quality blockers, and mitigation plans

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, secure resources, and maintain strategic alignment across the project. They make key approval decisions and help ensure work delivers business value.

### Responsibilities
- Define business outcomes, priorities, and success criteria
- Approve project initiation, budget, and resource commitments
- Review milestone progress and major trade-offs
- Escalate organizational blockers or competing priorities
- Communicate project outcomes to leadership and broader teams
- Provide final sign-off for major releases or changes

### Goals
- Ensure the project stays aligned with business goals
- Maintain executive visibility and decision-making velocity
- Support sustainable delivery with the right resources and sponsorship

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and milestone reviews
- Ad-hoc escalation and decision requests
- Release announcements and business impact summaries

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Sets direction alongside Product Managers and the Project Manager
- Depends on developers and QA/Testing Lead for reliable delivery signals
- Works with Project Managers to resolve prioritization conflicts and risk escalation

---

## Scrum Master / Delivery Lead

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Delivery Leads help the team work effectively by facilitating agile practices, reducing blockers, and protecting the delivery flow. They keep teams focused on value while supporting continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove impediments and coordinate resolution paths for team blockers
- Track team health, delivery flow, and iteration cadence
- Coach the team on agile practices and working agreements
- Support risk visibility and escalation to the Project Manager when needed

### Goals
- Improve team efficiency and predictability
- Reduce unnecessary context switching and delivery friction
- Create a healthy environment for iterative delivery

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Team health and delivery cadence updates
- Escalation notes with PMs and Product Leads when required

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to align delivery planning and reporting
- Partners with Product Managers to maintain backlog clarity and sprint readiness
- Supports Developers by reducing blockers and improving team coordination

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define the technical direction, evaluate design trade-offs, and ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with organizational standards. They guide technical decisions across the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review and guide technical design and solution architecture
- Evaluate trade-offs across system design, dependencies, and integration points
- Ensure alignment with platform conventions, security standards, and maintainability goals
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation or redesign strategies
- Mentor developers and review implementation choices during delivery
- Participate in planning for complex or cross-team initiatives

### Goals
- Enable sustainable, scalable technical solutions
- Reduce rework by improving design clarity early
- Maintain architectural consistency across the portfolio

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture decisions
- Planning sessions for complex features or dependencies
- Risk registers and architecture trade-off discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Developers to validate technical quality and implementation choices
- Works with Product Managers and Stakeholders to balance technical feasibility and business priorities
- Partners with Project Managers to identify dependencies and sequencing risks

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment activities, verify release readiness, and ensure production changes are communicated clearly and managed safely.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release planning, deployment windows, and staging validation
- Verify pre-release requirements are satisfied before launch
- Manage deployment checklists, smoke tests, and rollback readiness
- Draft and communicate release notes to stakeholders and support teams
- Track post-deployment verification and incident follow-up

### Goals
- Execute low-risk, well-coordinated releases
- Minimize deployment disruption and production incidents
- Ensure stakeholders understand release scope and timing

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and readiness meetings
- Deployment updates and release notes
- Post-deployment verification reports and incident coordination

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with QA/Testing Lead to confirm release readiness and validation coverage
- Coordinates with Project Managers on deployment timing and risk communication
- Partners with Stakeholders and Sponsors on release communication and business impact

---

## Security / Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads ensure projects meet required security standards and compliance expectations. They protect the organization by embedding secure practices and incident readiness into project delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements, review points, and compliance checkpoints
- Configure and review security scanning, dependency checks, and CI controls
- Review findings and recommend corrective actions for vulnerabilities
- Participate in incident triage and security escalation workflows
- Ensure the project follows relevant policies and governance standards

### Goals
- Reduce security risk across delivery and production environments
- Support secure and compliant deployments
- Enable rapid and informed incident response when needed

### Typical Communication
- Planning reviews for security requirements and controls
- CI/CD and issue tracking reviews for vulnerabilities
- Incident response updates and compliance follow-up

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Developers and Technical Architects on secure design and remediation
- Supports Project Managers with risk tracking and escalation paths for security issues
- Coordinates with Stakeholders and Sponsors on policy, exposure, and business impact

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles clarify accountability for delivery, quality, security, communication, and governance across the project lifecycle.
